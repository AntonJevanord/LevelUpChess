# Version 17 closeout

Version 17 closes at **`v0.17.23`** on branch **`version_17`**. Its nineteen
playable profiles retain their original definitions, including Knight pawn
`pawn.orange@3` and Cataphract `rook.purple@12` (`RC`). The final panel, earlier
experiments, generated profiles and research runtime identities remain intact.

The [final panel review](v17-final-panel-review.md) records 21,120 finalized
games and 21,106 scored color partners across 64 opening cases and three search
budgets. Keep Vanguard, Unicorn, Crusader and Amazon as the four frozen
level-three references. Nightrider and Wraith retain exploration status.

The selected revisions were tested separately during version 17. Their
**playable introduction belongs to version 18**, after the branch split:

| Design | Immutable ID | Movement | Tested engine MG / EG |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| Quiet-tall Knight pawn | `pawn.knight-tall-quiet@1` | `ifmnDfmWfceFfsNfmN` | 536 / 592 |
| Cataphract | `rook.cataphract-no-tall-back@1` | `RsCfC` | 2240 / 2413 |

Use new version-18 profiles and campaign declarations. Preserve all historical
profiles and keep these search priors separate from purchase costs and fitted
material estimates. The Cataphract revision does not become a fifth frozen
reference, and neither revision inherits its predecessor's experiment games.

Version 18 starts with roster activation and regression checks, then bounded
mixed-army validation using fixed level-one, revised level-two and original
level-three references. Runner performance work remains separate from balance
evidence; existing campaigns retain their original two-engine protocol.

The completed laptop engine-sharing benchmark showed no throughput improvement
for sharing in its small repeated-position workload. Retain separate engines
as the default while investigating aggregation and central pair dispatch.
